# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want AlgoVerse to be a place where anyone can learn or teach algorithms
without being made to feel unwelcome — regardless of experience level,
background, identity or the fact that they still reach for a nested loop when
a two-pointer would do.

## Expected behaviour

- **Assume good faith.** Corrections are welcome; condescension is not.
- **Be specific.** "This trace looks wrong at step 118, here is the input" beats "this is broken".
- **Explain the why.** A review comment that teaches is worth ten that just demand changes.
- **Credit generously.** Ideas, algorithms and prior art belong to the people who wrote them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
publishing private information, and dismissive gatekeeping ("just read CLRS")
are all grounds for removal from the project.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any
space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report anything uncomfortable to the maintainers via a private issue or email.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, remove comments, or
ban contributors, and will explain the decision to the person affected.
